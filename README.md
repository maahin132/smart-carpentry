# Smart Carpentry

> A professional digital platform for woodworking, furniture planning, material estimation, cost control, and project management.

Smart Carpentry is being developed for carpenters, furniture makers, interior contractors, woodworking businesses, and customers who need a practical way to plan projects, estimate materials and costs, optimize sheet cutting, and prepare professional quotations.

The platform brings project planning and estimation into one structured workflow while keeping the experience simple, precise, and business-focused.

---

## Overview

Woodworking and interior projects often involve manual measurements, material calculations, pricing changes, wastage, labour estimation, and quotation preparation.

Smart Carpentry aims to simplify this process through a centralized platform where project requirements can be measured, planned, calculated, optimized, and converted into professional estimates and quotations.
